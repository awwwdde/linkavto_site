================================================================
  INSTRUKCIYA DLYa TESTA EMAIL NA SERVERE
================================================================

1. ZAGRUZI FAYLY NA SERVER CHEREZ SCP:
   - test_email_server.py
   - avtolink/settings/production.py (obnovlennyy)
   - avtolink/settings/development.py (obnovlennyy)

2. PODKLYUCHIS K SERVERU PO SSH:
   ssh root@89.23.97.97

3. PEREIDI V PAPKU PROEKTA:
   cd /root/  # ili kuda ty zagruzhaesh

4. USTANOVI OKRUZHENIE PRODUCTION:
   export DJANGO_ENV=production

5. ZAPUSTI TEST EMAIL:
   python3 test_email_server.py your@email.com
   
   Ili interaktivno:
   python3 test_email_server.py

================================================================
ESLI NE RABOTAET:
================================================================

1. Proverte nastroyki:
   python3 check_email_settings.py
   
   Dolzhno pokazat:
   - Okruzhenie: PRODUCTION
   - Host: localhost
   - Port: 25

2. Proverte dostupnost SMTP:
   telnet localhost 25
   
   Dolzhno podklyuchitsya (Ctrl+C chtoby vyyti)

3. Vernte starye nastroyki:
   
   Otkroy: avtolink/settings/production.py
   
   Izmeni:
   EMAIL_HOST = '31.31.196.47'  # vmesto localhost
   EMAIL_PORT = 25

4. Proverte logi Django:
   tail -f debug.log
   
   ili
   
   tail -f /var/log/linkavto/django.log

5. Perezapusti Django:
   systemctl restart linkavto
   # ili kak ty obychno perezapuskaesh

================================================================
POSLE USPESHNOGO TESTA:
================================================================

1. Dobav v systemd service peremennuyu okruzheniya:
   
   nano /etc/systemd/system/linkavto.service
   
   Dobav v [Service]:
   Environment="DJANGO_ENV=production"

2. Ili v ~/.bashrc:
   echo 'export DJANGO_ENV=production' >> ~/.bashrc
   source ~/.bashrc

3. Perezapusti servis:
   systemctl daemon-reload
   systemctl restart linkavto
